# Scope

weekflow helps a project owner prepare and record weekly client meetings. Before scope approval, a docs meeting reviews source documents and records answers to open questions. After approval, a build meeting shows the work, gathers change decisions and previews their effect on the delivery date. When a build week is locked, weekflow offers the owner a project-docs update for agreed requirements that still need a citation in the PRD.

The owner checks and confirms the offline meeting page. The tool records the answers and attendees, makes a client PDF from the saved meeting content and locks the week once the PDF and any required paper signature are recorded. Open work carries forward. Dated corrections document mistakes in locked weeks, and the confirmed delivery history can be handed to checkflow.

The owner approves the page and sends the PDF. Scope approval stays with the project's meeting form, and signatures stay on paper. The tool runs offline and keeps the meeting record in the project folder. See the [guide](../README.md) and [security rules](security.md).
